## Note Structure

A useful technical note should capture:

1. Concept
2. Explanation in my own words
3. Mathematical or technical foundation
4. Example
5. Implementation, where appropriate
6. Common mistakes or limitations
7. Related course
8. Related book or paper
9. Further questions

Notes are for understanding and synthesis, not simply copying source material.
