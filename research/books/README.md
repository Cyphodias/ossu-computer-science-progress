The **36 primary books/references** in the research roadmap. These are the supporting texts used alongside the paper sequence.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Mathematics | **Introduction to Probability** | Blitzstein & Hwang | Probability/statistics | Before ML |
| Mathematics | **Convex Optimization** | Boyd & Vandenberghe | Optimization | During ML |
| Mathematics | **The Elements of Statistical Learning** | Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman | Statistical learning | During classical ML |
| Algorithms | **Introduction to Algorithms** | Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest & Stein | Algorithms spine | Core CS |
| Algorithms | **Algorithm Design** | Kleinberg & Tardos | Algorithmic problem solving | Core CS |
| Algorithms | **Concrete Mathematics** | Graham, Knuth & Patashnik | Mathematical maturity | Alongside algorithms |
| Architecture | **Computer Organization and Design** | Patterson & Hennessy | Architecture foundation | Before OS |
| Architecture | **Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach** | Hennessy & Patterson | Architecture depth | After fundamentals |
| Operating Systems | **Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces** | Arpaci-Dusseau & Arpaci-Dusseau | OS spine | Before systems papers |

| Machine Learning | **Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction** | Kevin Murphy | Modern probabilistic ML | During ML |
| Machine Learning | **Hands-On Machine Learning** | Aurélien Géron | Implementation | During ML projects |
| Deep Learning | **Deep Learning** | Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville | Deep learning theory spine | Before/alongside DL papers |
| Deep Learning | **Dive into Deep Learning** | Zhang et al. | Implementation + math | Alongside DL |

| AI Systems | **Programming Massively Parallel Processors** | Kirk & Hwu | GPU computing | During AI systems |
| Research | **The Craft of Research** | Booth et al. | Research method | Throughout |
| Research | **How to Read a Paper** | Keshav | Paper reading method | Throughout |
