# Research Reading Tracker

This is the high-level control surface for the research library. Detailed reference metadata and links are maintained in the papers index, while the staged learning path is in the Reading Sequence.

## Status Definitions

- ⬜ **Not Started** — collected but not yet studied.
- 🟡 **In Progress** — currently being studied.
- ✅ **Completed** — studied sufficiently to record as complete.
- 🔁 **Reviewing** — revisiting after initial study.
- 📚 **Reference** — supporting material retained for later use.
