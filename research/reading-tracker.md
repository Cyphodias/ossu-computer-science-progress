## Status Definitions

- 📚 Reference — collected for future reading
- 🟡 Reading — currently being studied
- 🔁 Review — being revisited
- ✅ Completed — studied and understood sufficiently to record
- 🧪 Reproduced — supported by an implementation or experiment
